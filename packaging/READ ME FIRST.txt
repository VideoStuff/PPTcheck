PPTcheck - PowerPoint technical auditor
=======================================

PPTcheck checks .pptx files for videos (length, size, frame rate, resolution),
transitions, animations, add-ins such as Slido or Mentimeter, and fonts that
need installing. Everything runs on your own computer: no files are uploaded
anywhere, and you don't need to install anything else.

HOW TO START
------------
Windows:  double-click PPTcheck.exe
Mac:      double-click PPTcheck.app (move it to your Applications folder first)

Your web browser opens the PPTcheck dashboard. PPTcheck runs quietly in the
background: there's no separate window. Drop .pptx files or folders onto the
page to check them.

HOW TO STOP
-----------
Click "Quit PPTcheck" in the top-right of the dashboard. It also stops by
itself about 15 minutes after you close the browser tab.

FIRST-TIME SECURITY WARNINGS
----------------------------
PPTcheck isn't (yet) signed with a paid Microsoft/Apple developer certificate,
so the first time you open it your computer asks you to confirm:

Windows - "Windows protected your PC":
  click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Mac - "PPTcheck cannot be opened" / "Apple could not verify...":
  1. Click "Done" (or "OK").
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
     "Open Anyway" next to the PPTcheck message. Enter your password.
  If the Mac instead says the app "is damaged", open the Terminal app and run:
     xattr -cr /Applications/PPTcheck.app
  then open it again.

You only need to do this once.
